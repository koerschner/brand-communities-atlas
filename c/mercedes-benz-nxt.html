TL;DR

  • What it is: Mercedes-Benz's "home for blockchain-based digital art and digital collectibles", launched on 22 May 2023 and run by 0xNXT, a dedicated company Mercedes set up with its agency Omnicom and four NFT-native founders. Between June 2023 and May 2025 it released about eight collections: a generative-art drop with Fingerprints DAO, design-heritage "Icons" cards, IRL-minted gifts for top customers, a free Superdackel mint, an AI-art collaboration, and a free Base mint. A Discord and roughly 15–30-person holder events sat around them (X launch post; Paragraph).
  • Why people came back: each drop rewarded earlier collectors (free mint passes, discounts, first-wave access), and the team ran Discord mini-games with a season scoreboard and signed F1 prizes. Small IRL events took collectors inside Mercedes: the IAA, the AMG factory, the Manhattan showroom, and test drives for collectors.
  • What the company fed it: a steady, polite content stream (car-by-car X threads, essays, Discord Stages, podcasts and panels), the Mercedes design team and museum, F1 memorabilia, and an in-car app (MBUX Collectibles) that displays NFTs on the dashboard.
  • Biggest mistake: planning supply for a crowd that never existed. The first Icons era planned 2,694 pieces, opened with a broken mint site, sold 201 in its auction and 390 in total, and of the seven planned eras only three ever shipped. Holders got "collect for the joy of collecting" with almost no utility, and the brand sold to just 10 eligible markets.
  • The lesson: loyalty rewards, games and IRL perks can't hold a community whose shared object has no use and a falling price. A collector base of about 100–400 wallets needed a cheaper, smaller, utility-led design from day one, not a seven-chapter, 18,860-piece roadmap.

What the community is

Mercedes-Benz NXT was a sub-brand for "digital objects": the team avoided the word "NFT" (Timo Heuer, 6 Mar 2024). It was run by 0xNXT GmbH (Königsallee 92, Düsseldorf), the platform operator in its terms (terms). Its public faces were the founders Timo Heuer (m3ta.eth), Sebastian Ihler (tedium.eth), Felix Schubert (fellyo.eth) and Max Orgeldinger (maxorgel.eth) (site, Wayback Nov 2024; LinkedIn).

The "community" had these parts:

  • Official spaces: the X account (from May 2023), a Discord (opened 15 Aug 2023, X), a Paragraph/Mirror blog, a LinkedIn page, and later Farcaster (X, 9 Feb 2024). Holder roles ("Star Collector", "Symbiosis Collector") were verified in Discord.
  • Collections ("core", "satellite", "peripheral"): Maschine (June 2023), Eternities (IRL-minted for top customers, from Sept 2023), Icons Era of Luxury (Sept 2023), Superdackel (Feb 2024), Icons Era of Technology (May 2024), "Composition of colorful particles on black" (free on Base, 2024), Symbiosis with Roope Rainisto (Nov 2024), and Icons Era of Precision (free, May 2025).
  • Unofficial spaces: no fan-run space was found. Searches of Reddit (via Brave and the Arctic Shift archive) found no NXT threads. The large Mercedes fan Discords (8,700–12,000 members) are general car chats with no tie to NXT (discord.com invite listing).
  • Status (Nov 2026): defunct. Mercedes put NXT "on hold" on 18 Mar 2025 (X), and the X account went inactive on 10 Jun 2025 ("The posts will remain for now, as a record of what we built", X). The account bio reads "This account is currently on hiatus." nxt.mercedes-benz.com now redirects to that X profile (r.jina.ai fetch, Nov 2026), and the Discord invite returns "Unknown Invite" (Discord API, Nov 2026).

Origin and strategy

  • Why: "Collectors have engaged with and enjoyed all kinds of physical objects, from the vehicles themselves to model cars and memorabilia. Mercedes-Benz NXT aims to bring this collectibility to the digital realm" (Paragraph, 23 May 2023). The stated vision was "to create digital objects that stand the test of time" (Paragraph, 24 May 2023). Ledger Insights reported the aim as reaching "new customer groups" with "fresh experiences" (30 Aug 2023).
  • Structure as strategy: 0xNXT was "a dedicated legal entity" formed with "long-time creative partner Omnicom and a team of NFT enthusiasts". For the first time this let Mercedes "participate in this space with a long-term strategy and the necessary resources" (Paragraph). The Dutch press release describes 0xNXT as Omnicom's studio, developing NFT projects exclusively for Mercedes (Mercedes-Benz NL, 15 Jun 2023).
  • The "predictability" framework: the team published a map of core, satellite and peripheral projects because "collectors value predictability" (Paragraph). It also said, deliberately and for legal reasons, that objects "should not be purchased with future benefits in mind, but instead, be collected for the enjoyment of collecting" (Paragraph).
  • Champions: Chief Design Officer Gorden Wagener's design team made Icons, Eternities and Superdackel. He co-shaped Maschine (Upstream Gallery, 31 May 2023), and the Mercedes-Benz Museum helped pick the cars (Paragraph).
  • Context: NXT launched four months after Porsche's 911 NFT mint failed to sell out in January 2023 (NFT Evening), and it was explicitly separate from earlier Mercedes NFT activity: the G-Class NF-G and the Mercedes-AMG F1 team's FTX-era drops (Paragraph).

Trajectory: the peak came at launch, then a steady decline. The paid core shrank from about 1,000 Maschine buyers (June 2023) to 189 Luxury collectors (Nov 2023) to about 100 wallets for each later Icons era. The only broad reach came from free mints (Superdackel; the Base mint, whose token IDs exceed 15,000 on Highlight), and those were the least committed holders. Only 3 of 7 planned Icons eras shipped, and about 16% of the 1,886 Eternities were ever minted.

What keeps people coming back

  1. Loyalty that spans drops. Maschine and Eternities holders could claim a free Icons mint pass and use it in any later era (Paragraph). Icons holders got the first Superdackel wave (200 pieces). Earlier collectors got 30% off Era of Technology and Superdackel holders 15% off (NFT Plazas). The final era was a free mint gated to existing holders (X, 9 May 2025). This "your old token unlocks the next thing" chain was NXT's main retention device.
  2. Discord games and seasons. These included "Guess the car" (weekly rounds revealing the next era's models, Oct–Nov 2023; X), several "Treasure Hunts" (Feb, Apr and Aug 2024; X), and a Discord Drivers Scoreboard that ran a 2024 "season" tied to the F1 calendar. Points milestones unlocked Discord roles, and prizes included caps signed by Lewis Hamilton and George Russell and a model car signed by Gorden Wagener. Points reset for a 2025 season that never came (X, 8 Jan 2025).
  3. Status and recognition. Holder roles were verified in Discord. Threads showcased individual tokens by collector name ("Icons #41 collected by Pakwalker", X, 29 Sep 2023) and celebrated rare trait combos ("Double Erlkoenig"). A community vote picked which collectors' NFTs would appear in the MBUX in-car app's demo mode (Jan–Feb 2025; X).
  4. Access to the physical brand. Holder-only events were deliberately small. They included a guided tour of the Mercedes-Benz Open Space at IAA Munich for 15 Maschine collectors (Sept 2023, X), a token-gated get-together for 30 at the Mercedes-Benz Manhattan showroom during NFT NYC (Apr 2024, X), an AMG factory tour plus dinner at the AMG Private Lounge in Affalterbach (Jul 2024, X) and a test-drive program for Icons collectors in Germany (Sept 2024, X).
  5. Being heard. Discord Stages included the post-mortem after the Era of Luxury launch (Oct 2023) and one that asked holders for feedback on "potential strategic paths" (Jan 2024, X).

What was missing: NXT never built a reason for daily use. There were no streaks, no member-to-member utility, nothing the token did beyond showing up in a car app (one beta, E-Class, US), and by policy no "future benefits." Switching costs were nil.

The content engine

  • Long-form framing: six essays on Paragraph/Mirror in May–Aug 2023 explained the framework, vision, each collection and the era structure (Paragraph).
  • X as the main channel: 1,246 posts in two years. Each era got a numbered thread with one segment per car (history, engineers' nicknames, trivia). For example, the Era of Precision thread covered the C 112, the 190 E 2.5-16 Evo II and the 500 E "Velvet Hammer" (X, 16 May 2025). Every thread ended with an anti-scam footer ("We will NEVER do stealth mints").
  • Live and spoken formats: Discord Stages, Stages hosted in partner communities (e.g. Degenz, Feb 2024), podcasts (Edge of NFT with Timo Heuer, Jan 2024, link; FiftyOne Insights with Max Orgeldinger, May 2024, link), and panels (nft now Gateway Miami, Dec 2023, link).
  • Art-world placement: Maschine was shown at Mercedes' Carlsbad design center and museum (June–July 2023), at a Bright Moments gallery evening in Berlin (Aug 2023), at an AV performance at Digital Arts Zurich (Nov 2024), and in the Mercedes-Benz Art Collection's "PROTOTYPE" exhibition in Munich (Jan–Mar 2025) (X).
  • UGC: light. There was a Maschine contest hosted by JPG (July 2023), the MBUX vote (holders tweeting their own tokens with #MBUXCollectibles), and reposts of collectors' test-drive photos. No creator, template or remix program existed. Production (thousands of rendered Icons videos) was outsourced to Lightshape (Lightshape).

The content was good brand content: accurate, heritage-rich and professionally made. But it was broadcast to a small audience, and engagement fell from hundreds of likes per post in 2023 to about 10–25 in 2025 (X API).

People, programs and governance

  • Team: the four 0xNXT co-founders acted as community managers, hosts and spokespeople, with Omnicom behind them and Mercedes design and museum staff as content sources. Team size beyond the founders is not published.
  • Partners as distribution: Fingerprints DAO (art collectors) hosted Maschine. The second Superdackel wave (100 pieces each) went to six token-gated communities: Degenz, nft now, Rug Radio, Station3, Superplastic and Zen Academy (LinkedIn). Mojito built the later mint platform with card payments and on-demand wallets (NFT Evening), and youba supplied NFC card wallets for top-customer IRL minting (youba case study).
  • No ambassador, MVP or holder-governance program was found. Feedback flowed through Discord Stages and replies, and changes were made in response (see below).
  • Rules: participation was limited to the EU, Australia, Brazil, Japan, Singapore, South Korea, Switzerland, the UK, the US and Taiwan "due to regulatory and taxation requirements" (X).

Growth loops

  • Borrowed communities: Fingerprints DAO's collector base produced the only sell-out (Maschine), and the six partner communities filled 600 Superdackel slots. These were NXT's best acquisition channels, but both brought collectors loyal to art or to their home community, not to Mercedes.
  • Top-customer IRL minting (Eternities) turned dealer events in Singapore, Belgium and the Netherlands into wallet onboarding (X, 17 Jul 2024). It reached about 295 people in roughly 20 months, against 1,886 planned.
  • Product hook: MBUX Collectibles (announced at CES 2024) put NFTs on the dashboard of the new E-Class in the US (Mercedes-Benz USA media, 10 Jan 2024). It was a clever loop in theory, but it shipped in beta only, at the very end, and couldn't display Maschine (X, 30 Jan 2025).
  • Missing loops: no referrals and no fan-run SEO or Reddit presence, and earned press dried up after 2023. One observer noted that he "did not come across any news about the release of Mercedes-Benz NXT Icons in several major media outlets" (Hu Kenneth).

Evidence of business impact

  • Hard evidence: none. No Mercedes-Benz Group filing, earnings call or press release reports NXT revenue, users or ROI. The shutdown note cites "challenging" market conditions.
  • Company claims: in the 18 Mar 2025 post, Mercedes said NXT "has given us invaluable insights and has laid a strong foundation for future digital innovations", for example "the creation of 3D data of Mercedes-Benz's classic cars for the first time" (X). Lightshape confirms that Mercedes "digitized a large number of the icons for the first time" for the project (Lightshape).
  • Primary sales (my estimate from the figures above): Maschine about 240 ETH gross (≈$440k at June 2023 prices; the split with Fingerprints DAO and the artist is unknown), Era of Luxury at most about 39 ETH, Era of Technology at most about 14 ETH, Symbiosis about $43k, and free mints nothing. That totals well under $1M gross, against a dedicated company, an agency partnership, IRL events and design-team time.
  • Market reality: the Era of Luxury floor (~0.02 ETH) sits 80% below its 0.1 ETH mint price (OpenSea).

What they did right

  1. Took the long view on structure and was honest about it. A dedicated company with NFT-native founders and a public framework set expectations. Mercedes avoided the hype and "utility" promises behind many brand rug-pull accusations, saying to collect "for the enjoyment of collecting" (Paragraph).
  2. Launched with credible partners and sold out. Pairing with Fingerprints DAO and Harm van den Dorpel gave NXT art-world legitimacy and its only sell-out (X).
  3. Rewarded early supporters across every drop. Mint passes, discounts, first waves and a final free mint for holders meant nobody who backed the brand was left out. The final "Era of Precision" was effectively a thank-you airdrop to the remaining community (X).
  4. Responded fast and in public when things broke. After the botched Era of Luxury auction, the team held a Discord Stage within eight days and listed concrete changes (X thread, 4 Oct 2023): keep the mint open, cap supply at what sold, add credit cards, switch to fixed prices and reopen mint-pass redemption. After the Symbiosis USD-to-ETH bug, it rescheduled "to ensure a fair chance for all collectors" and had Mojito publish a post-mortem (X).
  5. Spent its real advantage: access to the physical brand. The factory tour, IAA, showroom and test drives were things no crypto-native project could offer, and they drew some of the account's best engagement in 2024 (X).
  6. Lowered onboarding friction. NFC card wallets for top customers, card payments, on-demand wallets, and calling them "digital objects" rather than "NFTs" (Heuer).
  7. Shut down gracefully. Mercedes announced the pause in advance, ran one last free drop, promised tokens "will of course remain in your wallets exactly as they are", and left the archive up (X).

What they did wrong

  1. Sized for a market that had already gone. In autumn 2023 the team planned 18,860 Icons across seven eras, with a 1 ETH Dutch-auction start (Paragraph), months after Porsche's failed mint. Era 1 sold 7% in its auction and 14% overall. Only three eras shipped, and the narrative ("seven chapters through the design heritage") ended at chapter three. Consequence: the "predictability" promise broke, and floors fell below mint.
  2. A broken first mint. Wallet-connection and mint-pass failures marred the Era of Luxury launch (X, 26 Sep 2023; Hu Kenneth), the Superdackel public phase was paused because of bots (X), and the Symbiosis launch was rescheduled after a pricing bug (X). Consequence: holders "felt unrecognized and overlooked" and confidence fell (Hu Kenneth).
  3. No utility by design, while the community asked for it. "There does not appear to be any stated utility tied to the ownership of these collectibles. This is something their community in Discord has been vocal about, with many great ideas floating around, but none yet to be adopted" (DRVN Culture, ~May 2024). The IRL perks and test drives came late and served 15–30 people at a time.
  4. Fragmented collections. Generative art, car cards, AI art, a dachshund mascot, logo spheres and a free Base mint each had its own contract and audience. Superdackel (696 holders) and the Base mint brought in free-minters who never showed up for paid eras, and the paid core stayed at about 100 wallets.
  5. Excluded most of the world and most of its own customers. Only 10 jurisdictions were eligible, and the top-customer collection depended on dealer events. Its share (about 16% minted) shows the dealer channel never scaled.
  6. The car hook came too late. MBUX Collectibles reached a US E-Class beta only in early 2025, weeks before the pause, and couldn't show the flagship art collection.
  7. Left the doors open on shutdown. The Discord invite now fails, and a look-alike "Mercedes-Benz NXT — Free Mint" site (mercedes-benz.nxt-collection.com) appeared in search results asking for names and emails. It was unreachable in Nov 2026 and is not affiliated as far as this research could tell. A dormant brand NFT name is easy to impersonate.

Member voice

Direct member posts are scarce: NXT had no subreddit or public forum, and Discord isn't archived. The quotes below come from observers, collectors' questions answered on X, and the team's own words, each labelled.

  1. Observer, after the first Icons mint: "Those who supported their previous collections felt unrecognized and overlooked. Mercedes didn't listen to their community, who expressed concerns about the price points and supply." (Hu Kenneth, 5 Oct 2023)
  2. Same observer: "Despite this disaster, user loyalty remains evident. Users continue to share their NFTs on Discord, genuinely interested in collecting rather than speculating." (5 Oct 2023)
  3. Same observer, on the team's messaging: "Avoid expressions like 'we are all newcomers to Web3,' as users may not find it reassuring." (5 Oct 2023)
  4. Car-NFT newsletter: "The lack of added value to the NXT Era Icons NFTs will likely ensure that their demand is relegated strictly to the most die-hard Mercedes fans." (DRVN Culture, ~May 2024)
  5. A collector asked why Maschine wasn't in the car app. The team replied: "As Maschine is neither [image nor video] and is directly rendered in-browser, it is excluded for now. ☹️" (X, 30 Jan 2025)
  6. Team, to a winner of a Discord game: "Looks great on your shelf and we're very happy we could contribute to your physical collection as well." (X, 20 Dec 2023)
  7. 0xNXT co-founder Max Orgeldinger's test for brand partners: "Show me your wallet." (FiftyOne Insights, 29 May 2024)
  8. Team, closing the project: "Market conditions for creative initiatives like Mercedes-Benz NXT have been challenging since our launch in 2023 … The past years have been a journey of pivoting, iterating, and evolving." (X, 18 Mar 2025)

Lessons for building a product community

Copy these

  1. Make every past purchase unlock the next thing (passes, first waves, discounts, holder-only drops). It was NXT's strongest retention tool and cheap to run.
  2. Give members access only your company can give: factory tours, test drives, the designers, prizes from your athletes. Small, token- or account-gated, recurring.
  3. Run seasons with points and roles (the Drivers Scoreboard) tied to a calendar your audience already follows. A built-in reset gives people a reason to come back.
  4. Fix failures in public within days: host a live session, publish what changes, credit partners' post-mortems, reschedule for fairness.
  5. Lower onboarding friction for non-natives (plain words, card payments, one-tap wallets) and borrow distribution from adjacent communities with gated allocations.

Avoid these

  1. Don't build a big multi-year content roadmap and supply plan before you've proven demand. Start small, sell out, then expand. An unfinished "seven chapters" story signals failure to everyone watching.
  2. Don't make the community's shared object useless on purpose. If legal limits on promises apply, invest in experiences that ship now, not "no future benefits."
  3. Don't scatter attention across many unrelated collections or audiences. Free mints inflate counts but don't create members; concentrate on one core group and one hook.
