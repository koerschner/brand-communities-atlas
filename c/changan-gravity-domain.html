TL;DR

  • What it is: 引力域 ("Gravity Domain") is Changan's owner app. It launched on 2020-06-14 as the "UNI星球" app for the new UNI (引力) premium sub-brand. Between 2024 and 2025 Changan made it the one app for nearly every Changan-badged car, shutting the 智慧云控 app (2024-06-30) and the 长安汽车 App (delisted 2024-11-30) and moving Oshan owners off the 长安启源 app (2025). In June 2026 it ranked #1 of all Chinese automaker apps by daily time per user (9.1 min, against a 4.1 industry average) and #10 by monthly active users (2.269M). The coordinator's premise checks out, with one correction: 36Kr's headline says "2025年6月", but the tables and body describe June 2026 (36Kr/Analysys).
  • Why people come back: first, the app is the phone key and remote control. Second, a dense points economy pays for nearly every action: 1 point for a daily check-in, streak "blind boxes", 5 points per share (25 a day), 600–900 points for featured posts, 20 points a day and 200 a week just for using the remote controls, 2,000 for verifying ownership and 150,000 for a referred purchase, at 100 points = ¥1. Third, regional owner clubs (车友会) run by elected presidents organize events through the app.
  • What Changan feeds it with: a ten-year annual fan gala (粉丝盛典) where the chairman and president hand out awards ("super referrer", "experience officer") and ¥49,999 car vouchers; launch theater in the UNI era (owners announced the UNI-K's price, owner-modified cars went on stage); a user brand, "伙伴+" (2022), and "withU" (2026); and a Chief Customer Officer, Ye Pei, who has championed the app since the UNI days.
  • Biggest mistake: Changan treated owners' control apps as a cost to consolidate, not as something they own. Three migrations in about 18 months removed features buyers had been sold (remote engine start and windows on the Eado PLUS, charging status for Oshan Z6 owners) and added splash ads in front of the car key. Earlier, a 2022 data-security change had removed remote camera and remote parking three months after the UNI-V launch that advertised them, and U币 points were expired without notice (2023-12-01). The iOS rating sits at 2.6★, and the July–August 2026 reviews are almost all 1★.
  • The lesson: the time-spent crown measures captivity as much as love. Changan pays owners to open the app and press controls, and it removed the alternatives. That buys minutes, but the 2021 fan community spirit (U星人, co-created cars) did not survive the move to a mass-market utility. Copy the owner-club structure and the gala ritual. Don't use migrations and ads to manufacture "engagement".

What the community is

Official spaces

  • 引力域 App (Android com.changan.uni; iOS id1510275218, first released 2020-06-14, developer Chongqing Changan Automobile; version 3.2.0 on 2026-10-08; 330 MB) (iTunes lookup). The store listing names five pillars: 资讯大咖 (news from "auto experts"), 社区圈子 (community circles: post, comment, like), 上门服务 (doorstep test drives and servicing), 积分商城 (points mall) and 福利活动 (online and offline perks) (App Store CN). Version notes mention a 车友圈 (owner circle), topic management "aimed precisely at campaign targets", theme skins, drafts, comment previews and a revamped car-control screen (App Store US listing). There is also a WeChat mini-program version (changan.com.cn).
  • In-app owner clubs (车友会). Owners verify their car, add the 车友会 module and either join a club or apply for official certification. On the predecessor 长安FAN / 长安汽车 App, certification required 50+ members (Autohome Q&A). Clubs have presidents (会长), vice-presidents, finance and event teams. Dealer-founded clubs must be registered with Changan's service company and use a uniform badge; the dealer has to be a five-star service outlet with 300+ service visits a month. The Guangdong Changan club grew from 5 people to 1,000+ certified members, with model chapters (Eado, CS75, CS55, NEV) and eight city chapters (Autohome Q&A). Members register for events in 引力域 and then send the screenshot to the president (Autohome, 2025-03-16). Clubs carry star levels; one member hoped his would go "from four stars to five" (Autohome, 2025-09-21). Dealers also run branded "引力V享Day" owner days (for example Zhengzhou on 2024-08-24 and Dongguan in December 2025) (Sohu reviews; Autohome).
  • IRL rituals: the Changan Fan Gala (粉丝盛典), held yearly in Chongqing since about 2016 (the 8th was in January 2024, the 9th on 2024-12-29 with "nearly 1,000" users, the 10th on 2026-01-07 with 242 users from 11 countries) (inabr; Sina Auto; CCTV); and in the UNI era a UNI星球 anniversary on 24 July, plus a "UNI星球体验空间" store in Chongqing's Guanyinqiao, where even the coffee and pastries came from owners (Sina, Aug 2021).
  • Brand social matrix: Weibo @长安汽车 3.916M and @长安汽车引力 3.414M followers, 10.7M across all accounts (June 2026) (Sina).

Unofficial spaces. Peer help mostly happens on Autohome model forums, Dongchedi, Douyin and complaint sites (Heimao, 12365auto). The independent changan.biz forum for microvan owners shows 1.39M posts and 119k members (changan.biz). Reddit barely matters: r/changan has 48 subscribers and 49 posts (Arctic Shift, Nov 2026). Deepal and Avatr, Changan's EV subsidiaries, run separate apps. No evidence was found of Changan staff engaging in the unofficial spaces beyond templated replies to complaints ("公司高度重视…").

Origin and strategy

Phase 1: UNI星球, a NIO-style "user enterprise" for a youth sub-brand (2020–2022). Changan gave its UNI premium series the Chinese name "引力" (gravity) in February 2020 (Sina Auto). The UNI-T launched in June 2020, and the app went live the same month. Ye Pei, then a vice-president, nicknamed owners "U星人" (people of planet U) at the November 2020 UNI Honor Night, where an owner co-designed, Honor of Kings-themed UNI-T "王者战车" was unveiled and a "UNI星球成长计划" (growth plan) launched (Zhihu; youcheyihou). At the UNI-K launch on 2021-03-28, president Wang Jun let an owner announce the price, and the creator of the "planet cake", an early member of the UNI partner programme, was on stage (Gasgoo). Ye framed the aim as making owners "合伙人" (partners) and making UNI "a lifestyle, an interest circle". The "UNI宠粉计划" (fan-pampering plan) promised "products, communication, perks and gameplay decided by you" (Sina, Aug 2021). It all sat on Changan's 2017 data platform (DDM) and its "一号工程" (Project No. 1: customer research and direct customer relationships).

Phase 2: the UNI brand fades and the app becomes Changan's catch-all (2023–2026). UNI sales peaked and then collapsed: UNI-T went from 6,000–7,000 a month before 2022 to three-digit months in 2024 (Bitauto, May 2024). In March 2024 Changan folded Oshan, UNI, CS, Eado and Lumin into a mass-market "长安引力" brand with a 1.25M-unit target (Sina/Beijing News, 2024-03-27). The app followed the brand. 智慧云控 closed on 2024-06-30 (163.com, Jul 2024), the 长安汽车 App was delisted on 2024-11-30 (糯玉米, Benben and the CS/Eado EV and hybrid variants stayed on it for car control) (IT之家/Sina, 2024-11-01), and Oshan owners were moved off the 长安启源 app in 2025 (12365auto, 2025-10-22). The listing still says "UNI星球" and "U币" on some stores, a fossil of the first phase.

Strategy now. Changan's user brand "伙伴+" (launched 2022-12-26 at the 7th gala; it succeeded the 2019 "知音伙伴计划") promises "new service, new experience, new relationship" (Sina blog, Dec 2022). The 2024 plan added the "千万车主合伙人计划" (ten-million-owner partner plan), a referral prize pool "立足2500多万长安用户" (built on 25M+ Changan users) (inabr). At the January 2026 gala, Ye Pei, now executive VP and Chief Customer Officer, announced "新连接", an "all-time, all-domain" communication ecosystem "through the official APP, social platforms and year-round offline events", and "新价值": a member-growth plan, a "百千万" plan and an ecosystem-incubation plan to turn users "from product users into brand co-builders" (cqnews, 2026-01-12). Chairman Zhu Huarong told the 2024 gala that "一号工程" meant direct-to-consumer operations, with executive livestreams and round-the-clock service (Sina Auto).

Trajectory: the community figures peaked in the company's 2021 UNI-era claims, and no later post or comment counts were found. The app figures grew in 2025–26 mainly because Changan moved its other apps' owners into it. Analysys credits the time lead to "预约试驾/保养、车友圈社交、积分商城" (bookings, owner-circle social, points mall). That is an analyst reading, not a measured split. No source separates time spent on social features from time spent on car control.

What keeps people coming back

  1. The phone key, and the friction around it. Owners open the app to unlock, start, cool or heat the car, find it and check doors (Autohome CS75PLUS owner, 2025-06-30). Many mid-2026 reviews describe loading spinners, Bluetooth keys stuck "downloading", forced restarts and a splash ad before the key screen (App Store CN reviews). Hypothesis: part of the "stickiness" lead is time lost waiting, not time spent enjoying. Analysys's metric can't tell the two apart.
  2. Points for using the product itself. The 2022 rules pay 100 points for the first use of any of seven controls (auto-park, locks, windows, A/C, find car, seats, engine), 20 points a day after that, and 200 points for using controls on five days in a week (32r, rules captured Jun 2022). That works out to roughly 1,400 points (≈¥14) a month for opening the remote. It turns the utility into a daily-streak habit.
  3. A dense check-in and task economy. Sign-up 20, profile 50, first original post 50, ownership verification 2,000; a 1-point daily check-in with streak-based blind boxes, a "big surprise" draw and make-up check-ins; 5 points per share (max 25 a day); 600 points for a featured (加精) post and 900 if it reaches the homepage (max 4,500 a day) (32r). One user estimated that doing every task earns "16,000–18,000 points a month", at 100 points = ¥1, and asked an open-source auto-check-in project to support the app (GitHub issue, 2022-01-01). Bots were already farming the economy.
  4. Referral bounties. The "引力者计划" (Gravity-er plan, ID upload required): 150,000 points (≈¥1,500) when an owner's referral buys, 100,000 for a non-owner, and 5,000 to the new owner (32r). Changan's showcase anecdote: "one owner has already referred friends to buy 20 UNI cars" (Sina, Aug 2021). At the galas Changan awards "超级推荐官" (super referrer) titles (Sina Auto).
  5. Belonging through clubs. Star-rated clubs, presidents, team uniforms, seasonal meetups of about 30 cars, annual dinners with prize draws ("期待抽个车模", hoping to win a model car), and group-buy discounts negotiated with dealers (Autohome, 2025-09-21; 0755car). This is the most genuine retention layer, and it is largely volunteer- and dealer-run.
  6. Identity (UNI era). The names "U星人" and "UNI星球", co-created cars and the user-announced price gave early owners a tribe. After the 2024 merger the identity became generic "长安伙伴".
  7. Switching costs created by the company. Retiring the alternative apps (智慧云控, 长安汽车 App, 启源 for Oshan) leaves owners no choice: "换来换去的车控app，没有一个好用的" ("they keep switching control apps and none of them work") (App Store, 2026-07-16).

No evidence was found of public leaderboards, member levels shown on profiles, or staff hosts with followings inside the app.

The content engine

  • Company-produced: the 资讯大咖 news feed (brand news, new models, "industry tips"), campaign topics with permission-gated targeting, OTA and maintenance pushes, and themed skins (App Store; downxia). Executive livestreams form part of "一号工程". The UNI brand's first auto-show livestream drew 850k viewers in an hour in 2020 (Sohu, Sep 2020). Weibo and Douyin accounts post often (Sina).
  • Member content, bought with points: featured and homepage posts are paid 600/900 points, and shares 5 points each. The 53M comments claimed in year one (about 45 per "U星人") look like incentive-driven volume, not organic conversation (an estimate from the company's own numbers).
  • Showcase UGC: owner-modified cars, owner performances and "ten-year user story" films at galas; the CS95 club president told three stories on stage in 2024 (Sina Auto).
  • Education: no how-to library, academy or webinar programme was found. How-to help comes from peers on Autohome and from dealers.

People, programs and governance

  • Executive sponsors: Ye Pei (UNI-era VP, now executive VP and Chief Customer Officer); president Wang Jun (2021–2025) and Zhao Fei (2026); chairman Zhu Huarong, who holds a "老朱杂谈 Boss直达" Q&A with fans at the gala. "UNI主理人" Han Tingting ran UNI's owner programme in 2021 (Sina, Aug 2021). No community-team headcount was found.
  • Service staff: a "24-hour dedicated butler" for UNI owners, credited with 17M+ service hours in year one (company claim) (same).
  • Recognition programmes: gala awards for 超级推荐官, 伙伴行动推广大使 (partner-action ambassador), 卓越体验官 (experience officer) and 公益大使 (charity ambassador). In 2024, four "启源共创大使" (Qiyuan co-creation ambassadors) won ¥48,888 in purchase benefits plus six "3DAY" co-creation sessions (inabr).
  • Club governance: volunteer presidents; certification thresholds; dealer-founded clubs registered with the service company and subject to service-volume criteria (Autohome Q&A).
  • Feedback loop: in-app feedback can be filed by model, with "online consultation" (App Store notes). In practice, complaints about missing features went unresolved for more than a year (Oshan Z6 owners "反馈客服…至今一直未更新", "told customer service and it still hasn't been updated") (12365auto).

Growth loops

  • Referral bounties (≈¥1,000–1,500 per sale in points) and the 2024 referral prize pool.
  • Bookings funnel: test drives and doorstep services inside the app.
  • Forced migration gave the app millions of mainstream Changan owners without having to win them (MAU of 2.27M in June 2026, against UNI's ~150k owners in 2021).
  • Gala and club stories feed Weibo and Douyin content.

Evidence of business impact

  • Hard (third-party): June 2026 Analysys ranking: 2.269M MAU, 9.1 min/day (#1), YoY time +22.1% (36Kr). It measures engagement, not revenue or retention.
  • Company claims: year-one UNI community numbers; "one owner referred 20 cars"; 17M butler hours; and Changan's first-ever above-average result in J.D. Power's 2024 sales satisfaction study (SSI), noted in its 2024 annual report, which has no app or community metrics (2024 annual report).
  • Counter-evidence: UNI sales collapsed despite the community (UNI-T at three-digit months by 2024) (Bitauto), and the app's rating is low. No retention, churn, NPS or referral-share figure was found.

What they did right

  1. Built the community into the purchase and the car key from day one. Bookings, verification and controls all live in one app, so community surfaces sit next to a daily need. The result is the #1 time per user (36Kr).
  2. Rewarded real product use, not only posting. Paying points for weekly control use is an unusual and effective way to form a habit (32r).
  3. Gave the community structure and status: certified clubs with presidents, star ratings and model and city chapters scale IRL belonging cheaply through volunteers and dealers (Autohome).
  4. Ran a ten-year IRL ritual with visible leadership: a yearly gala, the chairman's Q&A, owner awards and owner-made performances (CCTV).
  5. Strong co-creation theater at launch (2020–21): a user-announced price, owner-modified cars and an owner-supplied store café produced press and a sense of ownership (Gasgoo).

What they did wrong

  1. Removed advertised features after the sale. On 2022-06-30, citing data-security rules, Changan removed remote live video, remote 360° photos and remote smart parking from all three apps, three months after the UNI-V launch that advertised them. A collective complaint drew 93 participants and called it false advertising (autochat, Jun 2022). BYD and XPeng made similar cuts, but according to the complainant Changan offered no compensation, only a call from the dealer.
  2. Migrations that dropped features. The 智慧云控 shutdown left 2023 Eado PLUS buyers without the advertised "远程车管家" (remote unlock, windows, engine start). There were 100+ complaints, and a lawyer said it "涉嫌违反…合同约定" (likely breached the purchase contract) (163.com, Jul 2024). Oshan Z6 owners lost charging status (12365auto), and UNI-Z owners lost the electronic fence and remote photos in an update (App Store, 2026-08-13). Consequence: a 2.6★ rating and complaint-board trails.
  3. Ads in front of the key. Splash ads and a sales page on launch, plus pop-ups on the control screen: "垃圾app，启动页打广告，无法控车！" ("junk app: ads on launch, can't control the car!") (App Store reviews, Jul–Aug 2026). This landed during a June 2026 MIIT crackdown on splash ads, prompted by a safety scare involving a navigation app (EET China, 2026-06-24; Huxiu, Jul 2026).
  4. Silent points expiry. U币 earned over "more than a year" were wiped on 2023-12-01 without notice (Heimao, 2023-12-19), the same pattern as Geely's.
  5. Engagement bought, not earned. Points for shares, check-ins and featured posts invited auto-check-in bots (GitHub, 2022) and inflated comment counts. The UNI identity faded with the brand, and the 2021 "U星人" tribe left no visible successor.
  6. Support gaps: online support "一直处于系统错误状态" (stuck showing a system error), with no callback for days (Heimao, Apr 2024); refunds on in-app orders were slow (Heimao; 12365auto, 2022).

Lessons for building a product community

Copy these

  1. Put the community inside the daily utility. Make the screen people must open (key, dashboard, workout) the doorway to the social layer.
  2. Reward the core behaviour, not just posting. Points for using the product weekly build habit better than points for shares.
  3. Federate IRL belonging through certified chapters: volunteer presidents, thresholds, status tiers and a local partner (dealer or store) to host.
  4. Hold one big annual ritual with leadership on stage and award titles to members who refer, test and volunteer.
  5. Use launches for co-creation theater. Let members reveal prices or show their builds. It costs little and is remembered.

Avoid these

  1. Never remove a sold feature through an app migration. Consolidation saves the company money but spends owner trust, and it shows up in ratings and legal threats.
  2. Don't monetize the critical path. Ads before a car key (or any must-use screen) turn engagement into resentment.
  3. Don't mistake captive minutes or paid activity for community health. Track voluntary actions (club events, unpaid answers) and satisfaction alongside time spent, and give notice before changing or expiring points.

Score justifications

  • Retention mechanics 3: a rich habit-and-status toolkit (streaks, blind boxes, control points, club stars, galas), but mostly extrinsic and propped up by forced migration.
  • Content engine 2: an official news feed and paid featured posts; no education library or creator programme beyond points.
  • Member ownership 2: clubs are member-run, but owners have no say over the features and apps taken from them.
  • Staff investment 3: C-suite sponsorship for ten years and a 24-hour butler (UNI), yet poor app quality and slow support.
  • Business impact evidence 2: a third-party engagement ranking only; referral success is anecdotal and UNI sales fell anyway.
  • Durability 3: the app has lasted six years and absorbed the others; the UNI community brand did not.

Open questions

  • Is Analysys's 9.1 minutes inflated by load times, ads and unsuccessful Bluetooth key attempts? Session-level data would settle it.
