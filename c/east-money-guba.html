TL;DR

  • What it is: Guba (股吧, "stock bar") is East Money's forum. It launched in January 2006 with one discussion room for every listed security, plus rooms for sectors, topics and funds, and it sits beside live quotes, news and the trade button in the East Money app. It has led Chinese stock forums by a wide margin since at least 2008. In Jul 2009 it had about 9 times the daily reach and 23 times the page views of Hexun's forum, its nearest rival (IPO prospectus), and academics still describe it as "approximately 10 times larger than its closest competitors" (Int. Rev. Fin. Analysis, 2019).
  • Why people come back: money and mood, not designed rituals. Every holder of a stock has a room where the price moves live and thousands of strangers react to it. Members come back to vent, to read the crowd (many treat Guba sentiment as a contrarian signal) and to share a culture whose slang, such as "关灯吃面" (eating noodles with the lights off), spread across China.
  • What the company feeds it: the whole portal. News, filings, research reports, money-flow data and Q&A with listed companies are piped into each bar. A creator platform (Caifuhao) adds traffic, badges and tipping with no platform cut, and listed companies pay for IR reach. Recently the forum's posts have also become training data for East Money's "Miaoxiang" AI model.
  • Biggest mistake: governing a sentiment machine lightly and only after scandals. Anonymous phone-number accounts, rumors, "black mouth" stock touting, abuse and stunt posts have repeatedly moved prices. Regulators named Guba in 2015 (a fake acquisition rumor), 2024 (eerily accurate "prophecy" posts led to a cleanup) and July 2026, when state media's CCTV commentary blamed a vulgar Guba "bet" post for limit-up trading in Offcn Education.
  • The one lesson: attach the community to the object people already care about (here, one room per holding, next to the price), and the habit forms on its own. But a community built on emotion needs moderation, verification and quality signals early, or the noise and abuse become the brand.

What the community is

Guba is East Money's investor forum. On the web it lives at guba.eastmoney.com. In the East Money app it appears as the discussion tab on every quote page, and a standalone Guba app has been on the App Store since 10 June 2012 (iTunes lookup). Its structure has stayed the same since launch. Each A-share, B-share and H-share has its own "bar", searchable by code, name, title or author (prospectus). Later came bars for sectors and concepts, funds (基金吧), topics, and the "问董秘" (Ask the Board Secretary) channel, where investors put questions to listed companies (Baidu Baike; Q&A list). Each bar shows real-time quotes, K-line charts, filings, research, money flow and a bull/bear poll.

East Money Information runs it under one company-wide "community management centre" and one set of community rules (社区管理规定). The company is now China's leading internet broker. It reported 2024 revenue of CNY 11.6B and net profit of CNY 9.61B (STCN, Mar 2025).

Unofficial spaces: Guba is the "emotional" pole of China's retail investor internet. Snowball (Xueqiu) is the "rational" pole and Taoguba the short-term traders' arena (The Paper, May 2021). Sina, Tonghuashun and Guotai Junan run their own "stock bars" (Guotai Junan added one in 2021, per Baidu Baike). I found no meaningful English-language or Reddit community around Guba. Status today: active and still the default A-share forum.

Origin and strategy

  • Founder and timing. Founder Qi Shi (其实) was himself a Shanghai stock commentator and financial-media "big V" in the 1990s. He started East Money with CNY 3M, and the portal launched in 2004 and the company in 2005 (财经天下 via Tencent News, Oct 2024; company timeline). Guba went live in January 2006, just as the 2005–2007 bull market sent the Shanghai index from 998 to 6,124 and brought in millions of first-time investors. The same article says new investors "suddenly had a base."
  • Strategic role 1: traffic and stickiness for an ad-funded portal. The 2010 IPO prospectus says the communities "increase the site's traffic and user stickiness" and backs this with pages of iResearch tables showing Guba leading every forum metric. It also admits the risk that the traffic was not yet converting into advertising revenue (prospectus). The IPO proceeds were earmarked partly to upgrade the "股吧子系统", to build simulated-investing and contest systems, and to build an "investor questions" platform, which later became 问董秘.
  • Strategic role 2: the free top of a financial funnel. With a fund-sales licence (2012) and the 2015 purchase of Xizang Tongxin Securities, East Money turned the "portal + vertical channels + community" traffic into fund sales and brokerage (The Paper; timeline). Analysts call East Money.com, Tiantian Fund and Guba the "three entrances" that cross-feed traffic at low acquisition cost. Guba's "strong social attributes" lock in stickiness (Huajin Securities, Aug 2018). The 2024 annual report still lists "massive user resources" and "user stickiness" as its first core competency, and it names its own "intelligent financial community platform" as a completed R&D project (2024 annual report).
  • Strategic role 3: B2B. Through "Qiyetong" (企业通) East Money sells listed companies "strong traffic reach to small investors": IR accounts, custom topics, roadshows and site visits (Qiyetong).
  • Where it fits among rivals. Futu's co-founder Li Lei framed the market in 2021: "股吧比较偏向直接感性的表达，大家比较容易看到一些情绪和消息面的内容" ("Guba leans toward direct, emotional expression; you mostly see sentiment and news-driven content"), while Snowball has the rational, fundamental analysis. Futu built its own feed around verified trades to differ from both (Digitaling, Jan 2021).

What keeps people coming back

  1. One room per holding, next to the price. The core design choice. A Chinese retail investor who owns a stock has exactly one obvious place to talk about it, opened from the quote page they already check many times a day. Retention follows the market itself: a limit-up or limit-down day pulls people in. The 2009 data already showed this habit at work: users viewed 8–9 Guba pages a day, against 4–5 on Hexun's forum (prospectus).
  2. Emotional release and solidarity. The forum's most famous post is a single line in the Chongqing Beer bar on 15 Dec 2011, on the sixth straight limit-down day: "今天回到家，煮了点面吃，一边吃面一边哭，泪水滴落在碗里，没有开灯" ("I came home, cooked some noodles, cried while I ate, tears dripping into the bowl, the lights off"). It became "关灯吃面", China's shorthand for a market wipe-out (zh.wikipedia; original post). East Money's own creator-program launch post lists the lore: "义庄舞剑救散户" (a Guba poster as a folk hero rescuing retail investors), "关灯吃面", "光波预测" (the "light-wave" forecasts that came true) (Caifuhao, Mar 2022). East Money filed to trademark "关灯吃面" in Aug 2021 (Baidu Baike).
  3. Reading the crowd as a signal. Many members visit to measure mood, often as a contrarian indicator. One wrote: "怎样从股吧情绪来判断股票走向？反着来" ("How do you read stock direction from Guba sentiment? Do the opposite") (Caifuhao, Mar 2025). East Money productizes this with the popularity and surge rankings (人气榜, rank page) and a "fan characteristics" curve that splits followers into "铁杆粉丝" (following 5+ months) and "新晋粉丝" (newcomers). Traders analyze these as signals (Caifuhao, Dec 2024; "人气榜是反向指标", Mar 2026). The rankings themselves also keep people coming back.
  4. Light status systems. An "影响力" (influence) score shown as half-stars up to five, earned from replies and likes rather than followers (Baidu Zhidao, 2017). Researchers' scrapes also show a "吧龄" (bar age, account tenure) field (CSDN dataset). V verification is available, and individual professional verification requires 100+ followers and 5+ long posts in 30 days (criteria). There are also follows and notifications.
  5. Contests and simulated portfolios. Public simulated portfolios start with CNY 1M of play money and come with profit leaderboards (group.eastmoney.com). The live-money "实盘炒股大赛" (real-account trading contest) advertised a CNY 100M prize pool, and entry required an East Money Securities account (contest; FAQ).
  6. Access to the companies themselves. 问董秘 lets any investor publicly question a listed company. It turns the bar into a pressure channel that members value and use (Q&A).
  7. Switching costs. Mostly network effects: the crowd for your stock is here. Follows and influence don't transfer, but I found no evidence that East Money uses them deliberately as lock-in.

The content engine

  • The portal is the content. Each bar automatically gathers 24x7 news, announcements, research reports, video and data, so a bar is never empty even when members are quiet (Baidu Baike).
  • Caifuhao (财富号), the long-form creator platform shared with Guba. Tipping passes to authors with no platform cut beyond a 1.2% payment fee (Caifuhao FAQ). The 星耀东方 (Xingyao Dongfang) creator incentive plan (Mar 2022) offers official recommendation and ranking exposure, topic and writing guidance, "one-to-one exclusive service and data analysis" and a custom badge. It asks for clear themes and "rational interaction" (launch post; guide). I found no disclosed creator counts or payouts.
  • Corporate and institutional content. Listed companies, fund houses and brokers run accounts. The 2023 roadshow series covered 150+ listed companies, nine online sessions and 400+ executives, and East Money produces video series such as 财富超现场 (on-site company visits) (Qiyetong).
  • AI. East Money says its Miaoxiang model draws on "百亿+股吧、财富号独家内容" (tens of billions of exclusive Guba and Caifuhao items). That claim comes from a user post, not a filing (Caifuhao, Mar 2026). The Guba app now offers screenshot-to-AI Q&A and AI explanations of ETF moves (App Store). The community's archive has become a proprietary data asset.
  • Missing: I found no structured education layer tied to the forum (cohorts, courses with discussion, staff-hosted rituals), unlike Futu's NiuNiu classes.

People, programs and governance

  • Team: no public community-team size or named community leads. Governance runs through a "社区管理中心" (community management centre) applying a long rulebook (rules). The rules ban unlicensed stock recommendations, "股市黑嘴" (stock-touting "black mouths"), fake securities information, contact details (to stop people being pulled into scam groups), paid post deletion, astroturfing, "恶意造梗" (malicious meme-making) and unlabelled AI content, and they provide an appeal channel. Penalties are tiered.
  • Superusers: creator incentives and verification (above). I found no volunteer moderator ("吧主"-style) program, which is a major difference from Baidu Tieba's model.
  • Feedback into product: no evidence of a structured loop. When an investor asked the board secretary in Dec 2025 to deal with rumours from accounts registered "with any phone number", the reply only gave a reporting hotline and email (Sina, Dec 2025).
  • Legal position: a 2023 Jiading court ruling cleared East Money of liability when Zhongke Yunwang sued it along with posters, because it had warned users, removed the posts and supplied account data (Tencent News, Aug 2023). That "notice-and-takedown" posture is the governance baseline.

Growth loops

  1. Bull-market inflow → forum → account. New investors arrive in rallies, find the bar for their stock and stay. East Money's Oct 2024 account-opening push told beginners they could "通过App里的股吧，和股友们在线交流讨论，获得帮助" (chat with fellow investors in the app's Guba and get help) (Tencent, Oct 2024). In 2021 the East Money app added 5.39M active users, equal to 27.5% of all new Chinese investor accounts that year (21jingji).
  2. Forum → monetized features. The standalone Guba app sells account opening and paid data add-ons (e.g., "T+0主力监控" at CNY 598) inside the community app (App Store). Trading contests require an East Money Securities account.
  3. Search and media pickup. Bars are public, indexed web pages, and Guba posts and memes routinely turn into national news stories.
  4. Listed companies bring holders. IR accounts and roadshows give companies reasons to push investors to Guba.
  5. Research and data ecosystem. Guba is the standard data source for Chinese investor-sentiment research (thousands of papers and paid datasets). That spreads the brand but doesn't directly recruit users.

Evidence of business impact

  • Hard evidence:
    • Guba's 2008–2009 traffic leadership is documented in the prospectus.
    • The 2007 leap (revenue CNY 67.35M and net CNY 34.87M, from CNY 4.12M and 1.10M in 2006) is attributed by 财经天下 to "股吧的洪荒之力" (Guba's raw power). The attribution is a journalist's claim; the numbers come from the company (Tencent, Oct 2024).
    • Revenue grew from CNY 0.61B (2014) to CNY 13.09B (2021) after the broker purchase, "靠东方财富、天天基金网、股吧等积累的流量池转化" (by converting the traffic pool built by East Money.com, Tiantian Fund and Guba) (Huxiu, May 2025).
    • The ~82% net margin in H1 2024 (Tencent) reflects low acquisition cost.
  • Company claims: "海量用户资源是本公司核心的竞争优势" (massive user resources are our core competitive advantage) (2024 AR). It does not split out Guba conversion. A widely shared figure of "18% conversion, CNY 35 CAC" comes from an unsourced user post (Caifuhao, Jan 2026) and should not be relied on.
  • Academic evidence on the community's market value:
    • Guba posts reduce stock-price synchronicity, meaning more firm-specific information gets into prices (Huang et al., Accounting & Finance 2023).
    • The effect is stronger after a 2013 anti-defamation law raised the authenticity of posts.
    • Posts are linked to higher firm productivity through better governance (Pacific-Basin Fin. J. 2021).
    • Guba sentiment also worsens intraday overtrading (arXiv 2024).

What they did right

  1. Matched the community's structure to the user's mental model. One bar per stock, launched at the start of a historic bull market, captured the category in 2–3 years. By Jul 2009 it had about 9x Hexun's forum's daily reach and 23x its page views (prospectus).
  2. Put the community inside the transaction flow. Quotes, news, data and the trade button sit around the discussion, so no separate habit is needed. That is why it became the top of a brokerage funnel.
  3. Filled empty rooms with company content. Automatic news, filings and research mean even thinly traded stocks' bars have something to read.
  4. Created two-sided value. 问董秘 and Qiyetong give listed companies a reason to show up and give investors access. The same infrastructure is also a revenue line.
  5. Productized the crowd's own signal. Popularity rankings and fan-tenure curves turn collective attention into data users return for.
  6. Let members' culture breathe. Memes born on Guba became national vocabulary, and East Money celebrates them (and trademarked one).

What they did wrong

  1. Rumours and manipulation, recurring for a decade.
    • In Apr 2015 a bank manager bought shares and then posted a fake "Hunan Development to acquire Caifu Securities" rumour in its Guba bar. The CSRC case was published by the CAC (CAC, Jul 2015).
    • May 2024: after "prophecy" posts timed to sudden price moves, East Money announced a special content cleanup with "梯级处罚" (tiered penalties) (Yicai; NBD).
    • Jul 2026: a vulgar "bet" posted in the Offcn Education bar pushed the stock to No. 1 on popularity rankings and to a limit-up open. The SZSE suspended accounts, the CSRC opened a case, and CCTV commentary told the market "莫把股市当赌场" (don't treat the stock market as a casino) (Sina/21st Century, Jul 2026; RCCaijing).
    • Consequence: regulatory and reputational risk tied directly to the parent's brand. Each fix came after the scandal.
  2. The popularity ranking amplifies stunts. The same rankings that drive return visits rewarded the Offcn post with top placement, which turned a joke into a trade.
  3. Low barriers and noise. Accounts need only a phone number (per the Dec 2025 investor complaint above), short anonymous posts dominate, and members and rivals describe the place as abusive and low-quality. That pushes serious investors to Snowball, and Futu positioned itself against Guba on exactly this point.
  4. Opaque moderation. Users complain that factual posts get deleted while touts and abuse stay up (Xueqiu user, Apr 2022).
  5. Scam funnel risk. Fraudsters use stock forums (Guba included) to pull victims into WeChat groups, as regulators note (Sichuan CSRC cases via Caifuhao, May 2025). East Money's rules and on-page warnings ban posting contact details, which shows the problem is endemic.
  6. Under-investment in quality and ownership. No visible volunteer-moderator program, no published community team, and a creator incentive aimed at traffic rather than quality curation. Growth has plateaued with the market (app MAU flat since 2021).

Lessons for building a product community

Score notes:

  • Retention mechanics 3: the habit is powerful but structural and market-driven, with few designed rituals.
  • Content engine 4: the whole portal plus creators and companies feed every bar.
  • Member ownership 3: members own the culture but have no governance roles.
  • Staff investment 2: no visible community team; moderation is reactive.
  • Business impact evidence 3: a clear funnel but no Guba-specific conversion data.
  • Durability 5: 20 years and still No. 1.

Copy these

  1. Make a room for every object your users own or track (here, every stock), and open it from the screen they already check. Then the community needs no separate habit.
  2. Auto-fill rooms with first-party content (data, news, updates), so early or niche spaces never look dead.
  3. Turn collective attention into a feature (trending lists, newcomer-vs-veteran ratios). Members come back to read the crowd itself.
  4. Bring the other side of the market in. Let vendors or partners answer questions in public; it creates value and a revenue line.
  5. Celebrate member-born culture. Memes and lore build identity cheaply.

Avoid these

  1. Don't let rankings reward provocation. If "most discussed" drives distribution, a stunt post can become an event. Add quality and verification signals before you add a leaderboard.
  2. Don't run a high-stakes community on phone-number anonymity and after-the-fact cleanups. Invest in verification, transparent moderation and appeals before a scandal forces it.
  3. Don't let the community's mood become your brand by default. A purely emotional space caps your audience: serious users leave for a rival ("Guba vs Snowball") and competitors position against you.
