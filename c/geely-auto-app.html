TL;DR

  • What it is: the 吉利汽车 App, relaunched on 2021-05-20 as the home of Geely's new user brand "我们" (Us, English tag "G FAMILY"). Its social layer comprises 吉友圈 interest circles, Q&A, a 吉分 points mall and owner-run "partner shops". The brand also runs an annual "Hi我们" user conference (first held 2022-08-06) and a "我们+" charity club. It has about 330万 monthly users, the #3 automaker app in China (June 2026), but those users spend only ~3.5 minutes a day there and the count is down 5.6% year on year (36Kr/Analysys).
  • Why people come back: mainly the phone key and remote control. On top of that come check-in and posting points, a points shop that even critics praise, and, for a small core, the annual conference and the owner identity the 我们 brand built (volunteers, owners performing on stage, owner businesses featured in the app).
  • What Geely did to feed it: a user-designed logo picked from 1,000+ entries, elected "user co-managers" (用户主理人) running the brand alongside a sales executive, owners voting on conference venues and headliners, a co-created owner magazine, 30,000 benefit packs claimable only after verifying ownership in the app, and an executive who calls himself "Chief User Rights Officer".
  • Biggest mistake: the participation story sat on a poor app. Owners went through three apps in three years (G-NetLink → GKUI → Geely Auto app). Remote control quietly dropped off older cars. A 2022 update removed key sharing without notice. Routine posts waited seven days for review. Then Geely moved model after model into a separate Galaxy app (2025), splitting the owner base it had just tried to unite.
  • The lesson: a well-designed identity layer (name, logo, rituals, volunteers) cannot make up for unreliable utility and gatekept speech. Fix the tool and let members talk before building the festival.

What the community is

Official spaces

  • 吉利汽车 App (Apple id1518762715, package com.geely.consumer). The listing describes a "我们板块" (Us section): "join 吉友圈 (Geely-friend circles) to swap ownership experience with other owners; take part in online and offline events…; get extra discounts at partner shops". It also has a Life/吉分 mall, vehicle status and remote control (doors, engine, windows, climate, Bluetooth key), maps, news and a showroom. Ratings: 4.4★ from 22,000, version 3.57.0 (2026-10-19), operated by Zhejiang Geely Holding Group, flagged for "messages and chat" and "user-generated content" (App Store CN). Its privacy policy names three Geely entities as joint operators and describes a "我们的伙伴" (Our Partners) process for owners to list their own shops (privacy policy).
  • "我们" user brand / "Hi我们" club. Announced 2021-05-20 and launched 2021-06-06/07 at Geely's China Design Center in Shanghai (12365auto; The Paper; Jiemian). Its Weibo account @吉利官方车主俱乐部Hi我们 has 110k followers. Weibo's own stats line showed one post the previous day with fewer than 1,000 reads (Weibo). "我们G Family" is the name of the owner-volunteer corps and of a WeChat official account that ran owner "story posters" (NetEase, 2022-08-18).
  • Sub-clubs: the "我们+" charity club (founded 2022-08-06), "我们的赛车俱乐部" (racing club, tied to Geely's 超吉联赛 series), and named owner clubs such as "动静的我们", "热爱的我们" and "向上的我们" (Autohome Q&A; Autohome 车家号, 2022).

Predecessor. The 吉行天下关爱俱乐部 (Geely "Care" club) was founded 2008-10-01 as a HQ-led network of dealer chapters, reported at 1,000+ chapters and 300k+ members (Baidu Baike via search snippet). In 2012 Geely's customer-relations head said it was run "HQ-led, chapter-operated", measured on join rate, activity and points, used mystery-shopper surveys, and had "never considered profit" (Sohu, 2012-05-09; Sohu).

Unofficial spaces. Most real peer support happens on Autohome model forums and self-organized WeChat/QQ groups. One Xingrui group claims to be Autohome's only certified club, with multiple 500-person WeChat groups full (Autohome). Reddit barely matters: r/Geely has 25 subscribers and 79 posts (Arctic Shift, accessed 2026-11-07). No evidence was found that Geely staffs these spaces, though executives say they read them (see below).

Relationship to Galaxy. The sibling report geely-galaxy-app covers the separate 吉利银河 app (2023–). From 2025 Geely began moving models out of the Geely Auto app into the Galaxy app: Xingyuan on 2025-04-10 and Panda on 2025-07-22, with orders, charging records and accounts carried over (Tencent News; IT之家). In November 2026 the fifth "Hi我们" was rebranded as the Geely Galaxy Global User Music Carnival (Sina/Weibo). The Hi我们 club brand now lives mostly on the Galaxy side, while the Geely Auto app remains the app for fuel and older models.

Origin and strategy

Geely had more than 10M owners but no direct digital relationship with them, and it watched NIO's founder-led community become the industry benchmark. Jiemian drew the comparison explicitly (Jiemian, 2021-06). Its answer was to make "the user" a brand of its own. Senior VP Lin Jie (林杰) framed it in six points: Geely had "been with users for 25 years"; "我们 is not just an app" but a tool to rewire internal decision-making "so users decide"; and users should be treated "as family" (42How, 2021-06-09). The governance design was unusual: user co-managers (用户主理人) elected by owners, a design advisory group and user designers, with sales executive Fan Junyi (范峻毅, nicknamed "范范") as the company's "co-manager" (Sohu, 2021-06; AutoLab).

The business logic, in Lin Jie's words: "Geely's first car is sold; the second is sold by service… a user's value is repurchase, re-purchase and referral." He also said Geely sets no KPI for user operations "because the car-sales chain is long" (TMTPost, 2022-08). In March 2022 Fan described the next phase: circles by topic, interest, city and model; a channel for owners' own businesses as a "nationwide mutual-aid chain"; and naming rights given to users. He said he personally "lurks in owner groups" and forwards issues to marketing (Tencent News, 2022-03-18; Autohome 车家号). Geely also tied this to an older co-creation tradition: the 2016 Boyue was named through a "全民定名" (name-the-car) campaign with 1.26M participants and launched with 5,000 "chief experience officers" (Geely Holding, 2016-03-28).

Trajectory. The app has large reach but shallow engagement and is now shrinking slowly. Analysys files Geely Auto under the "high-scale path… the app mostly plays 'digital key + basic query'", as opposed to the high-engagement apps built on community and services (36Kr). The QuestMobile and Analysys figures use different methods, so the January 2025 and June 2026 numbers are not directly comparable. No figures were found for posts, circle membership or active posters.

What keeps people coming back

  1. The key and the remote (utility). The app controls doors, engine, climate and the Bluetooth key. Owners relied on it enough that the 2025-04-29 Geely-group cloud outage stranded a Binyue family "in the wind and rain for 3+ hours" (Sina Finance, 2025-04-29). The flip side: for cars the app can't control (most pre-2021 models), there is little reason to open it.
  2. 吉分 points. Owners earn points by "publishing topics, sharing and commenting", plus a daily check-in, and spend them in the mall. No purchase is needed (Zhihu snippet; 6ziz how-to). The launch was seeded with 520 red packets of 1,314 points (a "520 / 一生一世" love pun) from CEO Gan Jiayue (ifeng, 2021-05-20). Even a harsh critic called the 吉分 mall "the most complete and best part of the whole app" (Hupu, 2022-01).
  3. Circles and Q&A. 圈子 (circles, "like WeChat Moments") and a Q&A board where "staff answer owners' questions and owners can answer each other" (ifeng). Fan Junyi cited the Q&A as the app's "real voice of users" (ifeng, 2021-06).
  4. The annual ritual. Lin Jie declared every user conference "a co-creation festival of 我们" (Yiche, 2022-08-10). Sites were Sanya "我们的音乐节" on 2021-10-10 (D&S case), Ningbo in 2022, Zhanjiang in 2023, Chengdu in 2024, Hangzhou in 2025 and Hangzhou in 2026. Owners vote on the venue and headliners and perform on stage. In Zhanjiang "every act… performers are all Geely owners" (Autohome 车家号). Long-time owners are honored on stage, such as one who had bought four Geelys since 2002 (same).
  5. Identity and belonging. The name 我们 ("us"), the owner nickname 吉友 ("Geely friends"), the G FAMILY wordmark, an owner-written rap "Hi 我们" and theme song "我们+", and the 8.6 charity day, where 86 signs "I love you" (Autohome 车家号, 2022).
  6. Status through contribution, not levels. Owners gain status by being picked as a G Family volunteer, featured in the magazine or on a story poster, or by running a partner shop. No evidence was found of badges, leaderboards or streaks beyond check-in.
  7. Switching costs: accumulated 吉分, the app-bound key and family sharing, and verified-owner benefits.

The content engine

  • Owner-story journalism. The co-created magazine 《你好，"我们"》 reached issue 2 in August 2022 and profiles owners' lives (Yiche). The 我们G Family WeChat account ran owner "story posters", and a hundred-plus owner posters were displayed at the 2022 conference (NetEase; Autohome 车家号). This is the community's most distinctive content, and it is about people, not product.
  • Co-created artifacts: the logo (1,000+ submissions, user-voted), the conference rap and the charity theme song.
  • Company channels: in-app news and campaigns, brand Weibo, Douyin and Xiaohongshu ("wherever users are, our channels must be") (Tencent News), plus event livestreams.
  • Education: none found. No how-to library, academy or webinars. Practical help (app bugs, Bluetooth keys, which app a model uses) lives in Autohome threads (Autohome).
  • Score 3/5: genuinely member-centred storytelling and co-created brand assets, but low cadence, little evidence it continued past 2023, and no useful ownership content.

People, programs and governance

  • Executive sponsors: Lin Jie (SVP, self-styled "首席用户权益官", Chief User Rights Officer) and Fan Junyi (sales GM and co-manager). Lin describes an internal 用户权益小组 (user-rights group) that is "active in forums and among owners" and takes complaints back inside (TMTPost; Autohome 车家号). No community-team headcount was found.
  • Elected user co-managers: two were elected at launch, with more promised "by field and category" (Sohu). No evidence was found of later elections or of what they actually decided. Treat this as unproven after 2021.
  • 我们G Family volunteers: the first owner-volunteer corps was recruited for the 2022 conference to handle partner-shop stalls, ticket recycling, merch and the story exhibit (NetEase).
  • Owner partner shops: owners' cafés, dessert and cocktail businesses listed in the app, and at the 2022 conference attendees redeemed meal vouchers at owner stalls (same). This is a rare example of a brand community sending business to members.
  • Car clubs: 100+ club representatives attended in 2022, carrying club light-boards. These descend from the dealer-chapter model of 吉行天下.
  • Moderation: posts and replies are pre-reviewed. One owner's 2021-12-31 post was approved only on 2022-01-07 (Hupu).
  • Feedback loop: "2,000+ suggestions" reached the app team. Lin: "the sharper the roasting the better" (TMTPost). Product co-creation claims (the "Energy Storm" design language, Boyue L naming) are documented as votes or inputs, but Geely overrode the most popular FX11 name, 星耀, in favor of "博越L" (same).

Growth loops

  1. Benefits gated on app verification: 30,000 conference benefit packs required owner verification in the app (8/6–8/12/2022). This is a cheap way to convert the legacy base into verified app users (Yiche).
  2. Co-creation as marketing: name-the-car and design votes bring prospects into the funnel, and launches piggyback on user conferences (Boyue L 2022, Galaxy E8 presale 2023, Xingyuan 2024/2025).
  3. Events to social: livestreams and owner posts, and since 2026, coordinated KOL hashtag waves (see the Galaxy report).
  4. Referral and repurchase are the stated goal, but no Geely Auto app referral mechanics or numbers were found. The detailed referral bounty belongs to the Galaxy app.

Evidence of business impact

  • Hard: third-party MAU of 289万 to 330万 makes it one of China's largest automaker apps by reach. Engagement is below the industry average (about 3.5 min against 4.1) and falling year on year (36Kr).
  • Company claims: a 2012 survey said member satisfaction and loyalty "rose markedly" (no numbers) (Sohu). Promoters link co-creation to Boyue's 2M sales (Sohu).
  • Missing: retention, repurchase or referral rates, NPS, and any link between the 我们 brand and sales. Geely Automobile's HKEX filings were not found to break out app or community metrics. Lin Jie's "no KPI" stance means Geely may not measure it at all. Score: 2/5.

What they did right

  1. Treating the community as a brand with members' fingerprints on it. The user-designed logo (1,000+ entries), elected co-managers and owner-voted conference line-ups made "我们" more than a slogan at launch (AutoLab; Yiche).
  2. Putting members on stage and on payroll-free business. Owners as performers, volunteers and partner-shop vendors. One vendor said she cleared her schedule to bring her mango ice to 1,000 attendees (NetEase).
  3. A cause members own. The 8.6 charity day and 我们+ club reached 15,000 members and 60+ events in 16 months, framed as Geely "resourcing owners who already volunteer" rather than branding charity (Autohome 车家号).
  4. Executives visibly in the loop. Named sponsors who say they lurk in owner groups, a user-rights group, and public acceptance of criticism (Tencent News; TMTPost).
  5. Using benefits to pull a 10M+ legacy base onto a verified app, then keeping a points mall good enough that even critics praised it.

What they did wrong

  1. App churn that abandoned older owners. Owners went from G-NetLink (late 2018) to GKUI (2019) to the Geely Auto app (2021), "three apps in three years". Different models needed different apps, and old ones stopped working (Hupu, 2022-01). A 2016 Boyue owner said G-NetLink "hasn't been updated in three years, so remote lock no longer works" (12365auto, 2021-01-22). Boyue Pro owners were told to pay for data to keep "standard" remote control (Heimao, 2024-11). Consequence: the oldest, most loyal owners, the ones the conference celebrates, got the worst app experience.
  2. Silent feature removals. A 2022 update removed car sharing "without any notice", leaving one-key families stuck (Autohome, 2022-08-03). Remote A/C disappeared after an update (Heimao). A Bluetooth key option was shown but unusable on one trim (Heimao).
  3. Over-moderation and exclusion. Seven-day post review, frequent logouts with image captchas, and no circles for the just-discontinued Emgrand GS/GL: "you tell us to use the app more while clamping down on us… are you pulling users in or turning them away?" (Hupu).
  4. Launching at scale without capacity. The new app went down for an hour on launch day under about 300k sign-ups (42How). On 2025-04-29 a group-wide cloud failure took down the Geely, Lynk, Zeekr and smart apps' remote control and Bluetooth keys (Xinhua 经济参考报; China Youth).
  5. Fragmenting the community again. Moving Xingyuan and Panda owners to the Galaxy app (2025), and then the Hi我们 conference itself (2026), split "我们" by powertrain sub-brand. The parent app is left as a key fob for the fuel fleet, which is consistent with its −5.6% YoY MAU.
  6. Governance promises that faded. Elected co-managers and owner-run business networks were announced loudly in 2021–22. No later evidence was found of them operating, and the brand's Weibo now gets <1,000 reads per post (Weibo).

Lessons for building a product community

Copy these

  1. Name the community and let members design its symbols. A user-designed logo and an owner nickname (吉友) create belonging cheaply.
  2. Give members jobs at your flagship event (volunteers, performers, vendors). Contribution creates deeper ties than attendance.
  3. Send business to members. Partner shops that feature owners' own businesses turn the community into mutual benefit, not just brand loyalty.
  4. Attach a member-run cause with a recurring date (8.6). It gives a ritual between big events and a reason to organize locally.
  5. Use one-time benefits to migrate a legacy customer base onto a verified channel, then keep the rewards store genuinely good.

Avoid these

  1. Don't build the community on an app that breaks or keeps getting replaced. Every app migration and silently removed feature tells the oldest members they don't matter.
  2. Don't pre-moderate ordinary posts for days, and don't leave legacy products out of the community taxonomy. Members will simply go to third-party forums.
  3. Don't announce governance you won't sustain, and don't split the community along internal sub-brand lines. Elected co-managers that vanish and a club moved to another app undo the "我们" promise.

Score notes

  • Retention mechanics 3/5: daily utility plus points, check-in, circles and an annual ritual, but engagement is below average and falling.
  • Content engine 3/5: distinctive owner-story magazine, posters and co-created songs, but no educational content and thin cadence after 2023.
  • Member ownership 3/5: user logo, elected co-managers, owner-voted events, volunteers and owner shops, offset by heavy pre-moderation and unproven continuity.
  • Staff investment 3/5: named executive sponsors, a user-rights group and costly annual events, against a long-neglected app.
  • Business impact evidence 2/5: third-party MAU only; Geely explicitly sets no KPI.
  • Durability 2/5: the club brand persists but has moved to Galaxy; parent-app MAU is down YoY and the Weibo account is near-dormant.
